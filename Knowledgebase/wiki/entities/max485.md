# MAX485-M

RS485 transceiver type named in [[sources/project-brief]]; couples [[entities/esp32-s3-wroom-1]] to the Daikin bus ([[concepts/bus-topology]], [[concepts/modbus-rs485-link]]).

## Chip facts ([[sources/max485-datasheet]])
- Half-duplex RS-485 transceiver, supply **5 V ±5 %**, 2.5 Mbps (far above the 9600 baud Modbus link), 1 unit load.
- Pins: 1 RO, 2 /RE, 3 DE, 4 DI, 5 GND, 6 A, 7 B, 8 VCC. DE high = transmit, /RE low = receive (p7, p10).
- Inputs accept 3.3 V logic (V_IH ≥ 2 V), but **RO outputs ≥ 3.5 V (5 V level)** while the ESP32-S3 is 3.3 V logic → level shift needed (inferred). See [[entities/esp32-s3-wroom-1]].
- 120 Ω termination at both bus ends; short stubs.

## Gaps
- The datasheet covers the bare chip family only. Whether "MAX485-M" is a breakout board, which components it carries (termination, level shifting, auto direction), and its logic/supply voltage are unknown — ask user / add module documentation to raw.
