# MAX485-M

RS485 transceiver type named in [[sources/project-brief]]; couples [[entities/esp32-s3-wroom-1]] to the Daikin bus ([[concepts/bus-topology]], [[concepts/modbus-rs485-link]]).

**Status (user decision, session 2026-11-27, not in raw):** this MAX485-M module is accepted **for the bench run only** (A/B open, no bus). For the on-site run and the deployment it will be **replaced by a MAX3485 (3.3 V) module** (user is buying; the on-site run waits for it). Reason: the module's DE and /RE pull-ups (below) break D8 on a live bus. See [[concepts/firmware-architecture]] section 6 row D23.

## Module facts ([[sources/max485-module-photo]], photo read visually)
- "MAX485-M" is a **breakout board** (small blue PCB), not a bare chip.
- U1 is an SO-8 IC with an **illegible marking** (re-confirmed by the `datasheet-reader` re-read 2026-11-27): chip type and manufacturer unconfirmed (MAX485 assumed from the name; MAX3485/SP485 not excluded).
- Logic header: **RO, RE, DE, DI**, with DE and RE on **separate pins**; no auto-direction circuitry visible *(inferred from absence on the top side)*. The firmware/wiring must drive DE and RE (or tie them together, see chip facts).
- Bus side: right header with "GND" readable (rest VCC/A/B *inferred*), 2-pole screw terminal (A/B *inferred*).
- Resistor designators (`datasheet-reader` re-read of the photo, 2026-11-27, image read visually; top side only, no traces visible): R1..R4 one beside each logic pin row, **RO = R1, RE = R2, DE = R3, DI = R4** (position high confidence; the electrical connection is *inferred*), codes tentatively "103" = 10 kΩ (low-medium confidence); R5/R6 tentatively "203" = 20 kΩ (low confidence); **R7 "121" = 120 Ω** (medium-high); R8 vertical near D1, code illegible, likely the LED series resistor *(inferred; revises the earlier "possibly bias")*. D1 possibly a power LED *(inferred)*.
- **Supply voltage not determined** from the photo (no 5V/3.3V marking visible). D23 planned the ESP board's 5 V pin; the bench run 2026-11-27 fed it from the board **3.3 V pin** instead (user: the 5 V pin "seems not working"; unverified whether it is only fed from the other USB connector). If U1 is a 5 V MAX485, 3.3 V is outside its 5 V ±5 % rating *(inferred)*.

## Bench measurements (B.2, user, multimeter, bare unpowered module, session 2026-11-27, not in raw)
Same readings with both lead polarities.

| Between | Reading |
|---|---|
| RO, /RE, DE, DI (each) – VCC | about 10 kΩ |
| RO, /RE, DE, DI (each) – GND | about 40 kΩ |
| VCC – GND | 30 kΩ |
| A – VCC | 20 kΩ |
| B – GND | 10 kΩ |
| A – B | 120 Ω |

Interpretation:
- **10 kΩ pull-ups to VCC on all four logic pins** (fits R1..R4 "103"); the 40 kΩ to GND is the 10 kΩ pull-up plus the 30 kΩ VCC–GND path *(inferred)*.
- **Bias network present** (A pulled towards VCC, B towards GND); exact topology and values *(inferred)*, fits R5/R6 "203" only loosely (low-confidence reading).
- **On-board 120 Ω termination fitted** (R7 across A–B). This resolves the earlier conflict, see below.

### Conflict (recorded 2026-11-07, resolved by measurement 2026-11-27)
- User statement, session 2026-11-07 (not in raw): "I had to buy a 120 ohm separately, it is not on the board by default." The wiki then treated the module as **unterminated** and left R7 "unexplained".
- Photo reading: R7 "121" = 120 Ω, *inferred* A–B termination ([[sources/max485-module-photo]]).
- **Resolution:** A–B = 120 Ω measured on the bare module (user, 2026-11-27): R7 **is** a fitted 120 Ω termination; the 2026-11-07 statement is superseded by the measurement. With this module the user's separate 120 Ω would not be needed at the node end *(inferred; moot, since the module is replaced for on site)*.

### Consequences (architect assessment, session 2026-11-27, not in raw; *inferred* design conclusions)
- **DE + /RE tied** = two 10 kΩ pull-ups to 5 V (5 kΩ) against the 10 kΩ DE pull-down (D8): DE ≈ 3.3 V whenever GPIO6 floats (reset/boot before the firmware drives it) -> **transmitter enabled -> violates D8 on a live bus**. At the 3.3 V bench supply DE ≈ 2.2 V, still above V_IH 2.0 V, so the issue persists.
- DI pull-up to 5 V injects about 0.1–0.2 mA into GPIO5 (outside the ESP32-S3 spec, minor); none at the 3.3 V bench supply.
- RO pull-up is harmless with the diode RX circuit ([[concepts/firmware-architecture]] section 4).
- A 10k/20k RO -> RX divider is **rejected**: with DE + /RE high RO is high-Z, RX sits at about 1 V (undefined) -> BADFRAME.
- The bench run only shows DE after firmware init; the **pre-init DE level is not covered** by the bench run.

## Chip facts ([[sources/max485-datasheet]])
- Half-duplex RS-485 transceiver, supply **5 V ±5 %**, 2.5 Mbps (far above the 9600 baud Modbus link), 1 unit load.
- Pins: 1 RO, 2 /RE, 3 DE, 4 DI, 5 GND, 6 A, 7 B, 8 VCC. DE high = transmit, /RE low = receive (p7, p10).
- Inputs accept 3.3 V logic (V_IH ≥ 2 V), but **RO outputs ≥ 3.5 V (5 V level)** while the ESP32-S3 is 3.3 V logic → level shift needed (inferred). See [[entities/esp32-s3-wroom-1]]. The bench RX circuit (diode + pull-up, static check PASS 2026-11-27) is in [[concepts/firmware-architecture]] section 4; at the 3.3 V bench supply RO is at most 3.3 V and the shift is not strictly needed *(inferred)*.
- Line terminated at both ends in its characteristic impedance; the 120 Ω value comes from the figures; keep stubs short (p13).

## Open
- **Replacement module (MAX3485, 3.3 V):** buying criteria, arrival tests and wiring (architect, 2026-11-27) are in [[concepts/firmware-architecture]] section 8: no pull-up on DE or /RE, no auto-direction, termination absent or switchable, strong bias or true fail-safe receiver. Bench B.1–B.7 to repeat on it before the on-site run (decision R2).
- Fail-safe idle with R7 fitted: **not sufficient on this module**. Bench 2026-11-27 (user, DC vs node GND, A/B open, 3.3 V supply): A 1.14 V, B 1.14 V, A–B 0.01 V, far below the ±200 mV receiver threshold (R7 collapses the bias, *inferred*); one boot latched the silence check with `activity 1` ([[concepts/firmware-architecture]] section 7 "Phase-1 bench run").
- U1 marking and supply rating of this module (illegible; moot if replaced).
- Where termination is needed on the node <-> "A A B B -" segment: [[concepts/bus-topology]] item 3.
